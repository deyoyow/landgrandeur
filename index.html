SHT-01 — The Company

GENERAL NOTES — READ WITH ALL SHEETS

Most firms hand your project from office to office. We built a company where the disciplines never separate.

Founded in 2023, MBS closes the gap between the drawing office, the construction site, and the server room. Engineers, designers, and software specialists work as a single team — so what gets designed can be built, and what gets built can be measured.

From infrastructure development to industrial optimization and specialized engineering projects, we carry one line of accountability across the entire arc: conceptualization, detailed design, project management, and construction supervision.
